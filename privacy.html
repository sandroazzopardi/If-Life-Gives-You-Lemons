About the Lemon Machine

100 original messages for a laugh, a gentle reminder or one small practical step. This is entertainment and everyday encouragement, rather than professional advice. Daily picks change at midnight UTC.

Privacy

This version does not use analytics, advertising, cookies, accounts or local storage. Messages and images are generated in your browser. Hosting services may process request information to deliver the site.

Sharing opens your device’s share menu, or copies the message and its link when supported. Saving an image creates a PNG on your device. Neither action sends your message to us.

Printables and purchases

Three sample cards are free for personal use. The full Little Zest Pack is not yet available to purchase. When available, checkout and delivery will be handled by the linked provider, under its published terms and privacy notice.

Printables are for personal use and gifts of printed cards. Digital redistribution and resale are not permitted.
