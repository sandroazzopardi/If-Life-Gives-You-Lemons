About the Lemon Machine

If Life Gives You Lemons is a small entertainment website offering original humorous messages, gentle encouragement and practical everyday nudges.

Choose a mood, squeeze the lemon and enjoy a message. You can also share a result or save it as an image. Daily picks change at midnight UTC.

The messages are intended for entertainment and general encouragement. They are not professional medical, mental health, legal or financial advice.

Information collected

You do not need an account to use the Lemon Machine. The website does not ask you to provide your name, email address or payment details to generate messages.

The hosting provider may process technical request information, such as IP addresses and browser details, to deliver and maintain the website.

Google Analytics

The homepage uses Google Analytics to help us understand how visitors find and use the website.

Google Analytics may collect information such as:

  • Pages viewed and time spent on the website.
  • The website or link that referred you here.
  • Browser, device type and operating system.
  • Approximate geographic location.
  • Interactions recorded by the analytics service.

We use these reports to assess the website’s popularity and improve the experience. We do not intentionally send names, email addresses or message contents to Google Analytics.

Google processes analytics information through its services. For more information, visit Google’s Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from sites that use its services .

Cookies and your choices

Google Analytics may use cookies to distinguish visits and measure website usage. The Lemon Machine itself does not use cookies or local storage to generate messages.

You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking analytics does not prevent you from using the Lemon Machine.

Google also provides a Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on .

Sharing messages and saving images

Sharing a result opens your device’s share menu or copies the message and its link to your clipboard, where supported. You choose where to share it.

The result link contains a message identifier so that another visitor can open the same message. It does not contain a personal profile or account identifier.

Saving an image creates a PNG in your browser for you to download. The website does not upload the generated image to a server.

If you share through another app or service, that service’s own privacy policy applies.

Printable cards

Three sample cards are available free of charge for personal use. The full Little Zest Pack is currently marked as coming soon.

You may print the cards for yourself or give printed cards as gifts. Reselling the cards or redistributing the digital files is not permitted.

Purchases

This version of the website does not accept payments. If the full printable pack becomes available, information about the checkout provider, delivery and purchase terms will be added before sales begin.

External links

The website may link to other websites. Those websites operate independently and have their own privacy policies and terms.
